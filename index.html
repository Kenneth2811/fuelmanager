Google Sheets connection

Not connected.

The Google Sheets link is built into this app. Everyone who opens the app connects and syncs automatically when it launches – there is nothing to enter or press.

Right now the link is saved only in this browser. To make every device that opens the app's web address connect by itself, click the green button, then upload the downloaded index.html to where the app is hosted (replace the old one). Anyone with the app's address will then be able to read and change the sheet's data.

The URL is saved in this browser and re-used after every refresh. Records added, edited or deleted here or directly in the Google Sheet are kept in step both ways; changes made in the sheet show up here within about 20 seconds.
